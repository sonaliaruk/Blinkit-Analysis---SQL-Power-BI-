# Blinkit-Analysis---SQL-Power-BI-

# 🚀 About PROJECT 

Blinkit (or any retail chain) wants to optimize sales and inventory planning  across different outlet types, sizes, and locations.
The goal is to identify sales patterns , understand how product attributes 🏷️ (like fat content, weight, visibility, and type) and store characteristics 🏪 (location, size, type) influence sales, and make data-driven decisions for:
* 🗂️ Stock planning and allocation
* 💸 Pricing strategies
* 👀 Product visibility optimization
* 🎯 Outlet-specific promotional campaigns
